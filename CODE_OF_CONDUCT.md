# Code of Conduct

## Standard

Everyone participating in this project — issues, pull requests, discussions, and
review — is expected to act professionally and in good faith.

Expected:

- Focus criticism on code, design, and evidence, not on people
- Accept technical disagreement without escalation
- Respect maintainer decisions on scope and direction

Not tolerated:

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language
- Publishing another person's private information
- Sustained disruption of discussions or review

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces and when representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns to sasan1200@gmail.com. Reports are handled privately.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and may block accounts, for
conduct that violates this document. Enforcement decisions are final.
