Product update

Shared inboxes now support routing rules

Published March 4 by the platform team

What changed

Routing rules send each conversation to the right queue before anyone opens it. Rules match on sender domain, subject keywords and the customer plan, and they run in the order you list them.

Limits

Up to 50 rules per inbox.

Rules apply to new conversations only.
